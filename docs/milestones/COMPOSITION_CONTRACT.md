# Experimental composition contract

7 November 2026. Mapping revision 2 / synthesis revision 3. Composition is disabled by default; the disabled branch retains the existing legacy action and PCM expressions. The scene v2 schema records these settings independently of the source timeline. Legacy v1 scenes retain their existing identities and disabled composition.

## Meaning and ownership

The worker compiles genuine timeline events into original musical motifs. Each resulting note retains its original event index. Event observations, identities, positions, uncertainty and schedule remain unchanged. Quantization and phrase offsets are explicitly performance-time transformations, never extra detections, movements, individuals or inferred animal behaviour. Unknown or unresolved taxa receive a neutral one-note fallback. The shark gesture is an explicitly nonvocal ecological metaphor.

The exact 32 supported taxa follow the existing factory catalogue and the NZ/global research dossiers. Motif contours, beat lengths, scale degrees, register, velocity and foreground/background roles are authored musical choices. They are not measured animal pitches/rhythms and no source recordings were auditioned for this implementation. Shared family primitives have distinct packets, contours and cadences where the written dossiers motivate them. Four notes cannot express complete natural repertoires.

The immutable plan owns numeric actions. Playback publication and retirement retain the existing serial-worker / callback-quiescence contract. Compilation may allocate, sort and throw; callback processing does none of these. Callback voices snapshot prepared pitch, envelope, gain, velocity and gate at onset; already ringing voices are unaffected by voice edits. No observation/provider objects, strings, I/O, locks or dynamic graphs enter processing.

## Controls and deterministic mapping

`CompositionSettings`: enabled false; scale majorPentatonic (or minorPentatonic, major, naturalMinor); tonicPitchClass 0..11; integer tempoBpm 40..180 (default90); density sparse/balanced/active; finite variation 0..1 (default .25); full uint32 seed (default1); quantize true. Invalid values fail worker preparation, including invalid enum casts. Settings are meaningful even while disabled and remain validated.

An event starts at its actual replay due time. Quantize rounds its performance onset forward to a quarter beat, using integer rational tempo arithmetic. Motif offsets/gates are authored sixteenth-beat ticks; tempo scales them. Density admits at most 1/2/4 events per absolute beat window and limits each motif to 1/2/4 notes. Variation uses a stable seed derived from source, event identity, revision and scene seed, never wall time, block sizes, STL random distributions or insertion order. Variation changes bounded degree, spacing and attenuation choices while retaining the first note's source-derived onset. Zero variation preserves each authored motif exactly.

Register roles are authored: lower foundation, middle background and upper foreground. Lower/background phrases are softer; phrase accents distinguish repeated notes. Notes are nearest scene-scale members in a bounded register, ties lower. Voice tuning is a transposition preference projected onto the same scene scale by 12 worker-prepared pitch-class multipliers; live tuning remains in-key. The high-register boundary uses the lower scale member when the nearest upper member would exceed MIDI88. This is deliberate compositional quantization, not natural-pitch measurement.

Final notes sort by performance due time, original schedule order and motif ordinal. A final absolute 10ms window admits at most four onsets, independently of host blocks; omitted generated notes are counted and events with no emitted notes receive a density decision. The original event index is retained for every note and decision. Each event emits at most four notes; a 200,000-note candidate/action hard cap throws before over-allocation. Timeline's existing 50,000-event cap remains. No overloaded note-on queue controls release.

## Voice envelope and resource bounds

Per-action velocity is bounded in (0,1], multiplying the existing at-most-.05 voice scale. A zero gateFrames preserves the legacy voice lifetime exactly. A composition gate sets the release start to min(attack+decay, max(attack, gateFrames)); the existing20ms raised-cosine release follows, while decay continues in the original update order. This preserves release continuity and prevents a gate cutting before attack completes. The original sweep timing/coefficients stay onset-captured; a shorter gate intentionally cuts the gesture through a smooth release.

Maximum authored base note is min(76, root+16), final tuned/scale-projected pitch at most MIDI88. Existing root48..72 and root+tuning48..72 validation remains. Composition spectral coefficient preparation uses the conservative MIDI88 maximum before sweep/modulation admission. Composition plan duration, phrase offsets and actual or editable-worst-case voice tails must fit30 minutes total; preparation fails instead of silently truncating an observation or note. Legacy disabled duration semantics remain unchanged.

Capacity stays eight primary plus eight retiring voices, existing513 bank slots and fixed callback work. The 12 pitch-class ratios enlarge each coefficient record by96 bytes plus a flag; no per-note ownership is introduced. Sample rate changes rebuild a plan. Stop, panic, seek and pause retain existing transport semantics; seek clears prior phrase/effect history and never reconstructs earlier notes. No new intentional DSP latency; quantization is an explicit score-time offset.

## Evidence and limits

Verification covers absolute expected motif times and scale notes, tempo and density effects, seed determinism, source index/order retention, key coherence with all32 initial presets and live tuning, gated release/velocity equations, legacy exact PCM, block partition parity, finite bounded output, duration/action limits and callback allocations. These numerical/ownership checks do not establish musical recognition, listening approval, physical device deadlines or DAW qualification.

The independent expanded-voice alias-sensitive probe now passes its scoped residual/convergence thresholds; see [probe](../sound-design/ALIAS_PROBE.md). The known prior pulse192k/32 benchmark misses are not repaired or waived here. Owner listening and representative mixed-scene evaluation remain required. Regional effects/spatial mixing are a later bounded milestone and do not silently become a graph here.

Research reused: [research/design practice](../sound-design/RESEARCH_AND_DESIGN_PRACTICE.md), [NZ taxon dossiers](../sound-design/species-nz-research.md), [global taxon dossiers](../sound-design/species-global-research.md), and their primary sonification/perception references. The design inference is that controlled timing, register and contour should be evaluated together with timbre; the literature does not validate these numeric motifs or make them natural observations.

Primary access for this milestone: the author-hosted [Grond/Berger parameter-mapping chapter page](https://sonification.de/handbook/chapters/chapter15/) was read, including its nearest pentatonic pitch-mapping example; its audio and linked PDF were not auditioned/read in this pass. This supports a transparent distinction between source data and a bounded musical pitch projection, not our particular motif values. The abstract, introduction and psychophysics setup of [Elhilali et al.2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2673083/) were read; their synchronous versus sequential grouping results motivate comparing staggered phrase gestures in mixtures. This is a design inference for future listening experiments, not evidence that these32 motifs are recognizable. No external implementation was copied.
