# Animal Atlas: release quality and product readiness audit

6 November 2026. Independent GPT-6 Astra review of the release areas outside the earlier architecture/data and audio/CPU audits. This report is a technical assessment and proposed acceptance plan, not a legal opinion or a claim of release qualification.

## Decision

Continue the isolated native instrument work, but treat **commercial-grade distribution as unqualified**. The existing prototype has deliberate protections and useful persistence semantics; it is not a packaged audio product. The highest unresolved decisions are a durable zero-cost distribution/licensing route, permitted production data, safe musical-session preservation, and qualification of the native/webview/host boundary. These decisions should precede promises of supported feeds or a downloadable production application.

The zero-cost requirement does not prevent professional engineering. It does constrain dependencies, distribution, support infrastructure and the available qualification matrix. A free download, a public endpoint, an unsigned local build and an apparently working DAW scan establish different things. Keep their evidence separate.

This report complements [the implementation audit](astra-build-audit-2026-11-06.md) and [the audio/CPU audit](astra-audio-cpu-audit-2026-11-06.md). Their session integrity, provenance, musical-action, realtime lifetime and DSP gates remain required. Do not count this report as repairing their findings.

## Findings and concrete remedies

### R1 — P1 decision blocker: choose a sustainable zero-cost dependency and distribution route

No JUCE dependency has yet been introduced, which preserves the option to choose deliberately.

The official JUCE repository offers AGPLv3 and its commercial licence. At this review the mutable `master` licence points to **JUCE 9**, so a future build must pin a release/commit and its actual terms rather than inherit a moving branch. [Official JUCE licence](https://github.com/juce-framework/JUCE/blob/master/LICENSE.md). The JUCE 9 EULA lists a free Starter tier subject to a US$20,000 annual revenue/funding limit, with different revenue definitions for individuals and legal entities. Eligibility, relevant contributors and distribution obligations need an owner decision; Starter is not an unconditional free-for-ever proprietary product strategy. The AGPL route is another choice requiring a compatible source/distribution plan. Do not select the owner's licensing model or assert compliance on their behalf. [JUCE 9 EULA](https://juce.com/legal/juce-9-licence/).

Normal Developer ID signing of macOS applications, plug-ins and installers is tied to Apple Developer Program membership, and Apple lists US$99 per membership year. This is a concrete conflict if the zero-paid constraint also excludes distribution fees. Eligible organisations can request a fee waiver; eligibility has not been established here. Local/source builds can continue, but an unsigned local launcher is not proof of a normal notarised download experience. Record the allowed distribution route before a release milestone assumes signing, and never silently add a subscription. [Developer ID](https://developer.apple.com/developer-id/), [program fee](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment), [fee-waiver requirements](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/fee-waivers/).

The **current VST3 SDK is MIT licensed**; avoid stale instructions that assume the older GPL/proprietary dual licence. Pin the actual SDK and preserve its notice. Steinberg treats trademark/logo use separately. This does not settle JUCE's licence or Animal Atlas's source/data rights. [Official VST SDK terms](https://www.steinberg.net/developers/vstsdk/).

**Gate:** a versioned dependency/asset register names the chosen project licence, exact dependency versions, provenance, notices, permitted distribution route and ongoing zero-cost assumptions. Include fonts, icons, globe geometry/textures, example data, JavaScript transitive packages, browser runtime and SDK components. No unverified source asset enters a release archive. No mandatory hosted service or paid fallback. A manual update/package workflow is sufficient initially; hosted accounts, automatic updating and commercial telemetry are not prerequisites.

### R2 — P1 shipping blocker: source access, reuse and sensitive-location behavior need enforceable capabilities

**Evidence:** `docs/source-registry.json:6–40` has null licences/request limits for both implemented sources; `animal_atlas/sources.py:147–154,214–217` explicitly reports unresolved reuse; `collector.py:9–12` nevertheless enables both in the current local-research defaults. This is documented prototype behavior, not evidence that either provider has approved a product. No source was contacted for permission by this audit.

Create a source/study capability record for each shipping integration: named upstream, exact access route, cost, authentication, request policy, approved use, local retention, display precision, export/redistribution permission, attribution, effective date and evidence URL/document. Treat data collection, bundling examples, event export, public screenshots and generated-audio distribution as separate reviewed uses; do not assume any outcome for derived audio. Preserve an unresolved state where permission is unknown. A product may support appropriately obtained user imports or permitted local material while a connector remains unavailable, but that is a different support claim from a cleared live feed.

Privacy scope extends beyond credentials. Station coordinates may reveal a contributor's location, named sessions may reveal a user's interests, and animal positions can be sensitive. The registry records a non-inference policy, but `model.py:48–58`, `sessions.py:13–20` and `ui/contracts.ts:21–40` have no typed restriction/precision envelope. Existing coordinates are passed through; this audit has not established a current restricted-location leak. Before more providers are added, represent provider redaction/delay, original precision, allowed presentation/export precision and the policy version explicitly. Never triangulate, interpolate or enrich a hidden position into a more precise one. Unknown accuracy remains unknown.

Apply the policy before coordinates reach visual DTOs, exports, diagnostics or future caches. A warning flag alone cannot prevent a disallowed export. Preserve permitted provenance while distinguishing provider claims from verified evidence. If exact data was never supplied, region assignment must not invent it. Clarify how later permission withdrawal affects new acquisition and existing local pieces; do not silently erase the user's compositions or promise indefinite use without reviewing the source terms.

**Gate:** a synthetic corpus containing redacted, approximate, delayed and absent locations passes through ingestion → history selection → map/inspector → region routing → bundle/CSV/diagnostic export without gaining precision or losing restrictions. Every default-on production source has dated access/use evidence; any unresolved source is accurately unavailable in that release. No original wildlife recordings, logos, media or data are bundled merely because a map displayed them. Product copy states independence and contains no implied affiliation.

### R3 — P2 demonstrated hardening gaps: local client redirects and invalid persisted tokens

**Redirect evidence:** `animal_atlas/client.py:18` disables proxies but uses the default redirect handler. Both GET (`23–24`) and POST (`39–41`) put the bearer token in ordinary request headers. The isolated probe confirms that this handler retains Authorization when constructing a request for a different authority. The current API does not redirect. Exploitation would require an unexpected/hostile endpoint, for example takeover of a stale local port; this is not a demonstrated remote-site bypass. The protocol already excludes defending against malicious code running as the same user (`docs/protocol.md:7`), but credentials should still never leave loopback through redirect handling.

**Repair:** reject redirects entirely for local IPC, validate descriptor protocol/store identity before use, reload descriptor/token on bounded reconnect, and check all response sizes and schemas consistently. GET has an explicit over-limit check; POST reads a fixed prefix without an explicit oversized-response distinction (`client.py:24–28,41–42`). Preserve the current no-proxy behavior.

**Token evidence:** `__main__.py:161–165` generates a token only when the file is absent, then trusts stripped existing contents. `api.py:64–75` compares against that value without a validity check. The method-level empty-token probe is accepted. A normal generated token remains protected; a corrupt/truncated token file is the prerequisite. Fail startup with a typed recoverable error, or atomically replace invalid state under exclusive ownership and reconnect clients. Never accept an empty/weak persisted token as a valid credential. Specify per-user file/IPC permissions separately on Windows rather than copying POSIX assumptions.

**Gate:** valid, missing, empty, truncated, malformed and inaccessible credential/descriptor fixtures; redirect responses for all supported status codes; stale descriptor and port reuse; unexpected protocol/store identity; excessive response and Unicode/type errors. There must be no outbound redirected request, token in logs/URLs/exports, or false successful mutation after an interrupted reply. These tests need only synthetic temporary state.

### R4 — P1 integration gate: the native webview bridge must be a narrow, validated privilege boundary

**Evidence:** `docs/native-react-architecture.md:62–74` already has the right intent. `ui/contracts.ts:107–119` is TypeScript-only proposed shape checking, not native validation; it has no stated payload/depth/in-flight limits, mutation idempotency or revision preconditions. `setTransport` returns a generation without requiring an expected generation (`96–98`). No bridge implementation exists to audit.

Before wiring React controls, specify request/version negotiation, bounded request IDs, allowed methods, native enum/type/range checks, exact int64 decimal parsing, message byte/depth/collection limits, maximum outstanding work and cancellation behavior. TypeScript types are not a security boundary. Reject duplicate/unknown keys according to the versioned schema. Imported/provider strings remain plain text; URLs require explicit schemes and safe external opening. Never interpolate source strings into JavaScript, HTML, shell commands or file paths.

All privileged browser content must be locally bundled and integrity-controlled. Deny unexpected top-level and frame navigation, downloads, pop-ups and permission prompts. Constrain resources and use a restrictive content-security policy compatible with the selected host. Do not expose filesystem/network/SQL/evaluation proxies. Native dialogs issue scoped file handles/tokens for chosen files rather than trusting arbitrary browser paths. External documentation/source links open outside the privileged view. Origin validation is required on messages and state delivery, including after navigation or browser recreation. These requirements align with Microsoft's explicit origin validation, structured messaging and narrow-object guidance; they remain to be validated in each selected JUCE backend. [WebView2 security guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/webview2/concepts/security).

For correctness, mutating requests need idempotency and expected state/session revision or a documented conflict policy. A late history/render completion must not replace a newer selection. A timed-out record-start retry must not create two recordings. Host automation, UI drags, preset loading and undo need one revision authority, with asynchronous state acknowledgement. Resynchronisation after editor reload updates the view without replaying notes. Native panic/audio disable must remain effective even when the browser or its request queue is stuck.

The existing HTTP protections should remain: loopback binding (`api.py:25`), exact Host and Origin checks (`64–68`), 8 KiB POST limit (`97–103`), 12 slots (`22,27–42`) and no-store responses (`59`). Twelve long polls currently consume all twelve slots. In a multi-instance product, reserve capacity or priority for control/status and bound subscription reconnects; do not simply raise the thread count without an aggregate resource budget.

**Gate:** malformed/deep/oversized JSON, arbitrary method names, bad int64s, NaN/ranges, injection strings, hostile source URLs, stale generations, duplicate requests, rapid cancel/reselect, bridge flooding, missing browser runtime and navigation attempts all fail safely. Verify in the real embedded backend, not only a browser mock. Closing/recreating one editor cannot stop collection or audio or invalidate another instance.

### R5 — P1 preservation gate: session export is not a complete backup, migration or retention system

**Evidence:** `store.py:19–53` enables WAL and initializes/upgrades schema automatically; `__main__.py:80–84` constructs that writable Store even for several library/read commands. There is no backup/restore command. `sessions.py:220–232` correctly uses a flushed temporary export and non-overwriting publication, but exports one frozen event session. It does not preserve the whole library, collection ledger, identities, active recordings or future musical assets. `store.py:89–92` checks capacity only on ingestion; session materialisation/import can grow the store (`sessions.py:113–116,266–272`).

Add a consistent SQLite backup operation using the supported backup mechanism, manifest and validation, not a copy of only a live main file. SQLite documents incremental online backup and the separate WAL state. [Online backup API](https://sqlite.org/c3ref/backup_finish.html), [WAL documentation](https://www.sqlite.org/wal.html). Bound backup disk use and interruption of writers. Publish only complete validated backups; label failures and leave the prior backup intact. Decide whether local backups include raw/restricted data and clearly distinguish them from shareable exports. Credentials should be excluded and re-established on restore.

Restore first into a new location, verify schema, structural integrity, foreign keys, session-envelope/content hashes and inventory, then offer a controlled handover. Never overwrite an active owner's database. Restoring an older backup can rewind sequences while retaining store ID: specify a new library incarnation or explicit cursor-reconciliation handshake so consumers cannot silently skip records or replay an entire baseline as live. This is a design risk that requires a test; no owner restore was attempted.

Migrations need explicit source/target versions, a tested consistent pre-migration backup, transactional/idempotent stages where appropriate, interruption recovery and an unsupported-future-version path. Read-only inspection should not implicitly upgrade a library. A product downgrade must refuse incompatible new state safely and explain recovery; an older binary must not rewrite it. Preserve original provenance through migrations and import, including the earlier audit's A2–A5 integrity/metadata fixes.

Separate retention classes: temporary response/cache data, raw evidence, durable normalized revisions/coverage, frozen pieces, render outputs and backups. Saved-piece dependencies are pinned until explicit removal. Cleanup needs previewable counts/bytes and must not remove an active recording's needed revisions. Quota admission covers finalisation, import, journal growth, backups and atomic-save temporary space, with reserved headroom to finish or mark interrupted. A shared data blob store is optional; do not add deduplication machinery before measured duplication warrants it.

**Gate:** crash/disk-full/permission-denied injection around ingest, finalise, export, backup, migration and publication; interrupted operations leave either the previous valid artifact or a clearly recoverable state. Restore into an empty temporary home with network/collector absent and compare all expected pieces/provenance. Verify restored-cursor behavior, corruption rejection, quota warnings and retention protection. Power-loss durability is a separate platform qualification from ordinary exception rollback; state the supported guarantee honestly.

### R6 — P2 reproduced usability defect: a library can outgrow its only listing route

`Sessions.list()` is limited to the newest 100 without a cursor (`sessions.py:177–181`); HTTP (`api.py:146–147`), native listing (`native/src/storage.cpp:93–99`) and proposed UI (`ui/contracts.ts:87`) mirror it. The fixture created 101 sessions; the oldest disappeared from listing but remained readable by ID. A user should not need a previously memorized UUID to recover a saved piece.

Add stable keyset pagination with a tie-break ID and search/filter semantics, including frozen/interrupted/recording states. Define concurrent insertion behavior. Keep bounded page sizes, and return a next cursor/end-of-list indicator. `latest` should query the latest eligible frozen/interrupted session directly instead of filtering a bounded mixed-state page (`__main__.py:85–89`).

**Gate:** 0, 1, 100, 101 and a large synthetic library; equal timestamps; new sessions arriving between pages; all IDs discoverable without duplicates/skips under documented pagination semantics; known-ID open remains available. No deletion or corruption was observed in this probe.

### R7 — P1 delivery gate: qualify the installed/offline artifact and its update lifecycle

**Evidence:** `Animal Atlas.app/Contents/MacOS/AnimalAtlas:1–3` opens Terminal and a neighboring script; `Start Animal Atlas.command:20–29` invokes installed Python. `docs/sessions-and-replay.md:7` correctly calls it unsigned and not a distributed runtime. `CMakeLists.txt` has no install/package target or GUI/plugin artifact. macOS arm64 source builds cannot qualify Intel, Windows, Linux or a clean recipient machine.

Define a small first supported matrix: named macOS versions/architectures, standalone format and eventually one explicitly tested VST3 host. Build universal binaries only when both architectures and all bundled components are qualified. Windows/Linux portability checks may run earlier without promising complete desktop support. Keep runtime requirements explicit: no Python, Node, development toolchain, source folder, CDN, web server or online asset fetch required for native playback/rendering.

Bundle the UI, fonts and legitimate globe assets. Natural Earth publishes its map data as public domain and is a concrete candidate for an offline basemap; this is not proof that any mockup or unrelated texture is reusable or geographically accurate. [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Record exact asset versions and simplify/validate geography deliberately.

Windows WebView2 must be detected and handled, including a clean offline machine. Microsoft's documented offline option includes the Evergreen Standalone Installer; Fixed Version distribution entails maintaining a browser runtime yourself. Choose and test the permitted redistributable and update policy, architecture and footprint. A tiny online bootstrapper does not satisfy offline installation. [WebView2 distribution](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/concepts/distribution). Linux requires a separately stated WebKit/system-library baseline; defer shipping it until that dependency story is qualified.

Use platform user-data locations outside the application bundle. Installation/update/uninstall must preserve user libraries, preferences and presets; removal of data is an explicit action. Collector ownership and version handshakes prevent an installer or new plug-in from starting a second writer. Update only after a safe writer handover, never mid-recording without an agreed recovery process. For an initial manual-update product, publish versioned packages and verification information; automatic updating is not required. If later added, authenticate update metadata/artifacts, verify before replacement, handle interruption/rollback and never download executable code from an arbitrary provider URL.

**Gate:** install as a standard user into a clean account/machine; launch without a developer environment; quarantine/signing/notarisation validation for the selected distribution route; open a packaged permitted fixture while offline; render/export; update from previous supported version; uninstall/reinstall without data loss. Include paths containing spaces/non-ASCII, read-only media, unavailable export folders, damaged/missing UI assets, full disk and absent webview runtime. Verify the **final packaged artifact**, not just the build-tree executable. No global OS security disabling should be a normal installation instruction.

### R8 — P1 audio-product gate: device and DAW lifecycle require separate qualification

**Evidence:** `docs/native-react-architecture.md:92,104` correctly says the wrapper, DSP and host behavior do not exist yet; `ui/contracts.ts:12–18` has only `audioAvailable`, not device/error state. The feature brief's audio-off/no-device requirement is at `313`; VST3 lifecycle requirements are at `335–346`. No real audio endpoint was exercised here.

Specify a standalone device state machine: unavailable, opening, ready, suspended, lost and failed, with one owner of device reconfiguration. Persist preferred device identity and fallback policy. Decide whether a lost device pauses musical time or advances silently; show that choice and do not replay accumulated old live events unexpectedly on return. Hot-plug/unplug, default-device changes, output routing, incompatible layouts, sample-rate changes, buffer changes, sleep/wake and device-busy failures must be recoverable without restarting the collector. Configure an output-only instrument unless an actual input feature requires microphone access; do not request irrelevant camera/location permissions.

For the plug-in, the DAW owns the audio device and host transport. Use the earlier audio audit's callback/lifetime/generation tests, then qualify plug-in scan/construction/destruction without network or a running collector. Test load/save/reopen, duplicate/delete instances, rapid editor open/close, resize, host automation gestures, bypass/suspend, loop/seek/preroll, missing tempo information, sample-rate/block/layout changes, offline bounce and freeze/flatten where the host supports them. Collector failure must affect live availability, not destroy frozen session recall. One shared collector must not stop when one instance disappears.

Decide portable DAW state: embed a bounded self-contained frozen piece or use content-addressed external assets with a reliable collect/relink operation. An absolute `.state` path alone is inadequate. Report missing/corrupt/newer-engine assets before producing a misleading successful render. Parameter IDs and state versions must survive upgrades. Lock product identity, plug-in IDs and parameter namespace before public projects depend on them.

**Gate:** named hardware/OS/device/host versions, qualified rates/buffer ranges, repeatable lifecycle scripts and release-build stress evidence. Include multiple instances with visible and hidden editors and control/collection load. Validate plugin format using available SDK tools, followed by actual supported-host testing; a validator pass is not an Ableton pass. Use already licensed hosts and free available tools; extra paid host licences are not implicitly authorised. Follow the owner's `.als`/`.amxd` Finder → right-click → Open With → **Ableton Live 12 Suite**, without `_updated`, rule for every host-session opening.

### R9 — P1 usable-release gate: accessibility and honest state transitions must be executable journeys

**Evidence:** the feature brief requires keyboard/focus/labels (`72`), color-independent markers (`89`) and the full empty/offline/partial/error set (`302–318`). These are design requirements only. No React runtime, accessibility tree or embedded-host focus behavior exists to assess; a polished image cannot pass them. The other agent owns Direction H visual design; these are integration gates, not a competing redesign.

Use WCAG 2.2 AA as a proposed measurable web-UI baseline, without presenting this audit as legal certification. Check keyboard operation, non-trapping focus, visible/unobscured focus, text and non-text contrast, names/roles/values, non-color distinctions, error identification and drag alternatives. W3C's minimum target criterion is 24 by 24 CSS pixels with specified exceptions; do not mislabel the 44-pixel AAA target as a mandatory AA rule. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).

Expose a list/inspector alternative to the WebGL map. Species, region and historical selection must be possible without locating a tiny moving point or drawing a polygon. Every knob needs a labelled numeric value with units, keyboard adjustment, reset and appropriate finer increments. Preserve focus/selection across Atlas/Sound Studio and modal close; test keyboard focus entering/exiting the plug-in editor inside the DAW. Test the actual accessible tree with VoiceOver on supported macOS; add the relevant screen reader on each subsequently supported platform. Automated browser checks alone are insufficient.

Use OS reduced-motion preference plus an in-app override; pause hidden globe animation, avoid mandatory animated travel and cap event announcements. Do not send every detection through a screen reader live region. Error/progress dialogs must remain cancellable and keyboard operable. Check display scaling, smaller resizable windows, long names, scientific names, Unicode text and text zoom. Unsupported dimensions get a stated usable minimum, not clipped essential controls.

Define complete journeys: first launch with no feed; permitted source setup; silent collection; history query with unknown coverage; empty/partial result; freeze → voice edit → save → reopen offline → render; device loss and recovery; corrupt/missing piece; cancelled load/render; unsaved edits on close; interruption recovery. In a history workflow, distinguish the requested calendar timezone and DST resolution from UTC storage, actual event extent, unknown coverage and playback duration. No events is not proof of ecological silence. Collection, playback and audio enable are independent controls and must remain visibly separate.

**Gate:** keyboard-only and screen-reader completion of these journeys in the real standalone and chosen DAW wrapper; contrast/semantic audits; reduced-motion and WebGL-failure fallback; state tests against real capabilities rather than mock fabricated feeds. Session sound edits need an explicit dirty state, undo/redo boundary and save/discard/cancel behavior. Destructive source-data/library removal needs an explicit reviewable action; routine knob edits do not need permission prompts.

### R10 — P2 operability gate: private diagnostics must distinguish failure causes without dumping user material

**Evidence:** `api.py:44–45,51–52` suppresses internal logs; `do_GET`/`do_POST` collapse unexpected errors into generic storage errors (`90–91,128–129`). `collector.py:62–72` uses safe provider/error categories, while the CLI exposes only exception class (`__main__.py:199–201`). These choices protect secrets but leave insufficient support evidence for diagnosing a production crash, schema mismatch, device failure or storage exhaustion.

Add bounded structured diagnostics off the audio thread: build/commit IDs, platform/architecture, component/protocol/schema/engine versions, monotonic timings, typed error codes, request correlation IDs, source attempt/success/freshness, resource/queue high-water counters, overrun/drop/group counts and device configuration. Distinguish user-correctable cases such as disk full, read-only path, unsupported version, corrupt session, unavailable feed, invalid response, missing runtime and device loss. Map them to clear actions in normal UI rather than exposing a stack trace or calling all failures storage errors.

Use rotating local logs with stated byte/time retention. Support bundle generation is a user-controlled export with a preview of categories and redaction. Exclude tokens, raw provider bodies, exact sensitive coordinates, personally identifying station metadata, session names/paths and event payloads by default. Hashing an identifier does not necessarily anonymize it. Full process dumps may contain secrets and animal locations; make them a separate opt-in artifact, not an automatic upload. Retain release symbols privately so a supplied crash address can be symbolicated against the exact build.

**Gate:** deliberately triggered failure classes produce the right message and support code; logs remain within quota under repeated failure; secret/location canaries never appear in the default support export. Crash recovery exposes a recoverable draft/piece without silently calling it complete. No paid telemetry or hosted crash service is required. A filesystem/IPC or UI crash must not masquerade as a healthy collector or a successful save.

Use a locked JavaScript dependency tree when UI implementation starts; build-time Node is acceptable, runtime Node is not required. Signing timestamps/notarisation artifacts can legitimately differ, so preserve pre-signing manifests and the hashes of final distributed packages. All applicable checks run with assertions effective, no unexplained skips, and project warnings treated as errors in qualification. Keep manual results traceable rather than claiming they ran in CI. Free runner quotas or available local machines bound automation; no mandatory paid runner assumption.

### R12 — P1 acceptance gate: define the product promise and support boundary before release copy

**Evidence:** README correctly describes a backend rather than a synth (`21`) and local launcher (`25`). Its statement that no collector was left running after validation (`27`) is historical evidence, not a statement about the owner's current state. The full feature brief distinguishes planned/implemented features (`348–366`) and accurately describes source limits (`278–285`). Preserve that discipline as the UI becomes convincing.

Define the first product as a named set of completed journeys and supported artifacts. Historical species + region + time with truthful coverage is an accepted requirement; it cannot disappear into a later wish list. Initial history can operate on permitted local collected/imported material while archive connectors remain unsupported. If worldwide live animal tracks are not qualified, say exactly which material/source classes are available. Animal detections are musical inputs, not population counts, real-time safety alerts, guaranteed animal positions or a source of original wildlife audio. No affiliation is implied by source attribution.

Document versioned file formats, source meanings, collection/sleep behavior, listening versus recording, safe save/restore, incomplete capture/coverage, supported devices/hosts/rates, offline limitations, engine-version behavior and known restrictions. Provide a short first-use path to a permitted offline piece and a clear explanation of silence. The quick start, screenshots and sample bundles must match the actual shipped capability manifest. State whether exact historical waveform recreation across upgrades/platforms is supported; reproducible event actions alone do not establish it.

Publish release notes and a compatibility/support matrix, a local diagnostic-export procedure, vulnerability-reporting route and maintenance ownership. An email address or repository issue workflow is sufficient if actually maintained; a paid service is not required. No perpetual support, uptime or data-availability promise should emerge accidentally from the interface. Freeze product identifiers and choose a project licence before public distribution; review the working title/branding separately rather than inferring name availability from this audit.

**Gate:** a person using only shipped documentation can install through the chosen distribution path, understand the data context, create and reopen a piece, recover from a missing feed/device, and export/restore correctly. Marketing and UI assertions have a matching qualification record. Unqualified capabilities remain unavailable rather than displaying invented success.

For Q0, use a deterministic provider simulator for repeatable disconnect/429/schema/slow-read conditions, plus a separately permitted live soak if justified. Include sleep/wake, clock/timezone change, low disk, collector restart and clients attaching/detaching. Metrics must distinguish system recovery, observation freshness and unknown upstream coverage.

Suggested order: settle D0 in parallel with headless audio; close S0 and build I0 before real UI control; complete S1 before native collection handover; prove one full offline composition/restore journey; then qualify the selected device/host/package matrix. A larger source catalogue, extra plug-in formats, elaborate globe shaders, many synth engines, cloud accounts and automatic updating should not displace these release gates.

## Decisions requiring owner/product input, without blocking independent engineering

1. **JUCE licence and exact version:** compatible source-distribution route, verified Starter eligibility/limits, or another zero-cost adapter. Do not presume closed source or open source.
2. **macOS distribution under zero-paid policy:** clarify whether a legitimate existing/fee-waived signing route is available, or limit distribution to an explicitly stated source/local-build route. No paid membership has been authorised or purchased.
3. **First supported product surface:** macOS standalone first is supported by the brief; identify the first exact OS/architecture/device/host support matrix and limits without implying all C++ platforms ship together.
4. **Data publication scope:** choose the actual permitted source/study routes and allowed output types once evidence exists. If no source is cleared, use permitted user material and labelled synthetic demo fixtures for engineering; never relabel them live.
5. **Preservation promise:** distinguish saved event composition, captured control performance and waveform compatibility; set the supported backward-read/engine-version policy and default retention/backup behavior.
