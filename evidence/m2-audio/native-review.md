# Independent native M2 review

Reviewer: GPT-6.1 SOL agent. Review date: 7 November 2026 (NZDT). This is an independent code/evidence review, not human listening, DAW, audio-device or hardware qualification.

Result: **no confirmed actionable defect found in the reviewed final implementation**. The previously reproduced near-end stop failure is fixed: draining reaches `clearToStart()` at either its fade endpoint or the finite plan endpoint. The regression remains enabled.

Inspected `native/include/atlas/audio.hpp`, `native/src/audio.cpp`, `native/src/wav.cpp`, the render/version paths in `native/src/main.cpp`, supporting Timeline and saved-session loading boundaries, CMake source identification, the M2 contract and build evidence. Reviewed the independent equation, transition, dense allocation/stealing, partition and invalid-input tests previously authored in this stream; their existence alone was not treated as proof of boundary correctness.

- Ownership/callback: renderer borrows a stable plan; the synchronous render worker owns both lifetimes. Construction, file writing and destruction are outside process. Callback scans fixed 8+8 voice arrays, performs at most eight admissions per sample, has no ownership retirement or dynamic containers, and bounds output blocks to 4096 frames. Controls are explicitly single-owner between calls. This does not provide a future cross-thread publication protocol.
- Admission/finite/rates: Timeline caps input at 50,000; plan rejects duration/settings outside its declared finite ranges. Absolute scaled windows retain four records before upward sample conversion. Two adjacent windows can merge into one sample, fitting eight admissions/retiring slots. Slot retirement occurs before the next sample needs capacity. Maximum oscillator partial remains below Nyquist at the lowest rate and highest mapped MIDI note. Finite lifetimes, attenuation-only master and non-finite recovery keep output bounded.
- Transport: generation checks precede mutation; pause advances through its fade then freezes, resume continues state with fade-in, stop suppresses admissions and clears to frame zero, panic clears immediately, and seek clears tails and lower-bounds future admissions. Inspected transitions at natural completion and the repaired stop endpoint, including repeated retarget behavior.
- RIFF/publication: header/chunk arithmetic agrees with the emitted 40-byte extensible fmt, fact, stereo 24-bit data and padded INFO comment. Maximum duration/rate limits keep frame arithmetic representable, and RIFF capacity is checked before writing. File bytes are explicitly little-endian. Exclusively created sibling staging plus hard-link publication refuses an existing destination, including a late competing destination; failures do not remove or overwrite that destination. RAII cleans only the staging path created for this operation under the normal cooperating filesystem model.
- Source traceability: load verifies frozen payload count/order/content SHA-256 inside one SQLite read snapshot before constructing audio; recipe uses the returned verified metadata. This is content integrity, not authentication of ecological truth or a cryptographic signature. The recipe records the engine source identity.
